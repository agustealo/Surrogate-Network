# **App Name**: Surrogate Companion

## Core Features:

- Profile Creation: Users create profiles detailing what they offer and what they need, divided into 'Offerings' and 'Requests'.
- Dynamic Needs Creator: AI-driven engine processes user descriptions of their needs in natural language, extracting key themes and suggesting 'Need Tags'. The engine functions as a tool that extracts needs and generates suggested tags.
- Multi-Dimensional Matching: Algorithm considers direct need-to-offering matches, contextual compatibility, and behavioral patterns to suggest potential connections.
- Granular Feedback System: Users rate their surrogate companion on specific criteria related to the fulfilled need, such as 'Punctuality,' 'Reliability,' and 'Communication Clarity'.
- "This for that" Surrogateship: A 'This for that' surrogateship offer, i.e., back rubs and empty trash, for daily hot meals.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft lavender (#E6E6FA) to evoke a sense of calm and balance, reflecting the supportive nature of the app.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F5F5) provides a neutral backdrop, ensuring readability and a clean aesthetic.
- Accent color: Muted teal (#70A1AF) to highlight key interactive elements.
- Clean, sans-serif typography to ensure legibility and clarity.
- Use minimalistic icons to represent various needs and offerings.
- The layout should be intuitive and emphasize the 'Offerings' and 'Requests' sections of user profiles, to reflect the app's framework for needs-based connections
- Color scheme: Blues (trust), purples (intimacy), and pinks (approachability).
